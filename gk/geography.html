World Geography

Introduction

Geography is the study of Earth's physical features, people, and cultures. Understanding geography helps us appreciate the diversity and complexity of our world.

Continents

1. Africa

  • Capital of Egypt: Cairo
  • Largest Country: Algeria
  • Highest Peak: Mount Kilimanjaro (Tanzania)
  • Notable Features: Sahara Desert, Victoria Falls, Great Rift Valley
  • Population: Over 1.3 billion
  • Countries: 54 recognized countries

2. Europe

  • Largest Country: Russia (European part)
  • Capital of France: Paris
  • Capital of Germany: Berlin
  • Highest Peak: Mount Elbrus (Caucasus)
  • Notable Features: Alps, Mediterranean Sea, Thames River
  • Major Union: European Union (EU)

3. Asia

  • Largest Continent by Area and Population
  • Capital of China: Beijing
  • Capital of India: New Delhi
  • Highest Peak: Mount Everest (29,032 meters)
  • Notable Features: Great Wall of China, Taj Mahal, Himalayas
  • Largest Country: Russia (Asian part)

4. North America

  • Capital of Canada: Ottawa
  • Capital of USA: Washington, D.C.
  • Capital of Mexico: Mexico City
  • Highest Peak: Denali, Alaska (6,190 meters)
  • Notable Features: Niagara Falls, Rocky Mountains, Grand Canyon

5. South America

  • Largest Country by Area: Brazil
  • Capital of Brazil: Brasília
  • Highest Peak: Aconcagua (6,961 meters)
  • Notable Features: Amazon Rainforest, Andes Mountains, Iguazu Falls
  • Popular Tourist Destination: Machu Picchu (Peru)

6. Oceania

  • Largest Country: Australia
  • Capital of Australia: Canberra
  • Notable Features: Great Barrier Reef, Outback Desert
  • Island Nations: New Zealand, Fiji, Samoa, Vanuatu
  • Population: Approximately 43 million

Major Bodies of Water

Oceans

  • Pacific Ocean: Largest ocean, covers ~165 million km²
  • Atlantic Ocean: Second largest, separates Americas from Europe/Africa
  • Indian Ocean: Borders Asia, Africa, and Australia
  • Arctic Ocean: Surrounds North Pole
  • Southern Ocean: Surrounds Antarctica

Important Rivers

  • Nile River: Longest river (6,650 km), flows through Egypt
  • Amazon River: Largest by discharge, flows through Brazil
  • Yangtze River: Longest river in Asia, flows through China
  • Mississippi River: Major river in North America
  • Thames River: Flows through London, England

Geographical Features

Mountain Ranges

  • Himalayas: World's highest mountain range (Asia)
  • Andes: Longest mountain range (South America)
  • Rocky Mountains: Major range (North America)
  • Alps: Important European mountain range

Deserts

  • Sahara: Largest hot desert (Africa) - 9 million km²
  • Arabian Desert: Second largest (Middle East)
  • Gobi Desert: Cold desert (Asia)
  • Kalahari Desert: Located in southern Africa

Rainforests

  • Amazon Rainforest: World's largest (South America)
  • Congo Rainforest: Second largest (Africa)
  • Southeast Asian Rainforests: Highly biodiverse

Climate Zones

  • Tropical: Hot and humid year-round
  • Temperate: Moderate climate with four seasons
  • Arid: Very dry with little rainfall
  • Arctic: Extremely cold polar regions
  • Mediterranean: Warm, dry summers and mild winters

Interesting Geographic Facts

  • Earth's surface is approximately 71% water and 29% land
  • The equator divides Earth into Northern and Southern hemispheres
  • Prime Meridian (0° longitude) passes through Greenwich, London
  • Mount Everest is the highest mountain at 8,849 meters
  • The Dead Sea is the lowest point on Earth at -430 meters
  • Antarctica is the coldest continent with temperatures reaching -80°C
  • The largest country by area is Russia
  • The most populous country is India (2023 onwards)
